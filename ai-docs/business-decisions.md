## Product risks (current, 2.0.4)
- Test churn: REQ-011, 020, 041, 083, 084, 101, 102, 103, 112, 120, 127, 128, 129, 133, 135, 136, 147, 148 are amended; their tests change with them. Keep old behaviour for projects that do not use the new data (rule: existing projects render unchanged).
- Format 11 refuses downgrades; users who go back to 2.0.3 cannot open projects saved with the new data.
- Hardware AV1/VP9 and preview decoding unverified on every system; the output check and fallback protect files, not speed. Speed claims stay unmeasured until Andrea's runs.
- Font additions raise every installer; Arabic and Devanagari fonts are the added size (cap 25 MB); CJK is not bundled (D61), so Japanese, Chinese and Korean text can show empty boxes on computers without CJK fonts (known limitation, note in the program).
- Per-clip aspect and clip speed add timeline and export complexity; regression risk on export graph.
- One window: data path to the running instance must not weaken REQ-057 (no writes outside folders; no crash on bad input).
- Autosave writes to cache every 2 minutes; large projects may cost disk time.
- Spatial sound rotation is a synchronous pre-pass (REQ-273): the preview waits for it, and clips over 600 s keep recorded sound there; an asynchronous pre-pass is future work.
- Translation: none in 2.0.4 (D60), so no translation-quality risk this release.
- Accessibility support may add cost to every frame; QG check needed.
- Earlier risks still open: stale splash summaries; Users expect new clips to get whole-video effects (now a notice, REQ-201); LUT colour differs from other tools; player scope creep (reduced by REQ-249..254); unsigned installers warning; Wayland drop; Faster can be slower on integrated graphics (REQ-207 explains it); REQ-177 not met (known limitation); manual checks of 2.0.3 not recorded (REQ-165, REQ-166).
