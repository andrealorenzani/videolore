New in this version
The Applied effects column sits on the right of the Preview; every effect except Speed can be moved up or down in it.
Whole-video Scale, Move and Apply LUT now give each clip its own copy; the whole-video popup is removed.
A text's size follows its box, with an optional maximum per text; LUTs are prepared in the background with progress.
Faster export checks its decode and samples at the same time; picture items are limited to 16 megapixels.
Projects are saved in format 13: 2.1.0 and older refuse them, and older projects open as before.
