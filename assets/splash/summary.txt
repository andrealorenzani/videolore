New in this version
Subtitles can be exported as WebVTT or ASS, imported from .srt and .vtt files, and kept as a separate track in the full video export.
Autosave every 2 minutes with crash recovery; drag clip edges and markers on the timeline; the [ and ] keys jump between markers.
Clips can run from 50% to 200% speed; PNG pictures can be placed on the picture; a clip can have its own frame shape.
360 lines keep their box and highlight, can be re-anchored, and show arrows when out of view; spatial sound follows the camera.
Faster export with AV1 and VP9; workspaces, themes and a customisable toolbar; Crop, volume keyframes and LUT strength.
