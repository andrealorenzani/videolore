New in this version
Enlarge word grows from the base of the word, and the words around it move apart; the line keeps its centre.
As subtitle track writes the lines as a separate switchable subtitle track in the export.
Volume is now Volume and track, with an audio track choice; Overlap media has its own row in the Effects list.
The Subtitles effect uses the clip's own audio and subtitle tracks; the blurred fill shows in the preview.
Projects are saved in format 15: 3.0.x and older refuse them, and formats 14 and older open as before.
