New in this version
Overlap media puts a PNG, GIF or MOV with transparency over a clip, with its own time and a box you drag in the preview.
Change speed and Volume are effects of their own, and Volume takes keyframes; each clip can be set to Fit or to an empty space.
Crop is removed: a crop in an older project is dropped when it opens, with one message.
Enlarge word keeps the other words in place; a stored Never start-screen choice now shows once per version.
Projects are saved in format 14: 2.2.0 and older refuse them, and formats 13 and older open as before.
