New in this version
LUT files up to 256 points and 512 MiB now open; large ones load in the background with progress.
Applied effects widget: move a LUT, text, picture or the subtitles up or down in the order they are applied.
Shadows for texts and subtitles; two word highlight colours; Enlarge word and Typewriter subtitle styles.
Projects are saved in format 12: 2.0.4 and older refuse them, and older projects open as before.
A new LUT or picture goes on top of the effects order.
