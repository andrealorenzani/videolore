New in this version
Subtitles can be exported as WebVTT or ASS, imported from .srt and .vtt files, and kept as a separate track in the full video export.
Autosave every 2 minutes with crash recovery; drag clip edges and markers on the timeline; the [ and ] keys jump between markers.
Faster export on more hardware: AV1 and VP9 presets, hardware decoding in the preview, and a choice of graphics card in Settings.
The window, popups and sections are remembered; workspaces, a customisable toolbar, themes and screen-reader names are new.
Effects: fade length, Crop, volume keyframes, Clean audio strength, LUT strength and favourite LUTs; 360 videos keep their marker in MKV and WebM.
