# Architecture: effects, order and the Applied effects column

Part of [Architecture](architecture.md). Requirements: [effects](business-effects.md), [effects and frame](business-effects-frame.md), [applied effects](business-applied-effects.md). Picture effects (frame, Scale, Move, LUT, overlaps) are in [picture](architecture-picture.md); subtitles in [subtitles](architecture-subtitles.md); the export of effects in [export](architecture-export.md).

## Applying an effect
- Each effect (Fade IN/OUT, Subtitles, Volume, Clean audio, Change speed, Apply LUT, Scale, Move, Change aspect, Overlap media, Add Text) applies **to one video clip** or **to the whole video**. Every way gives one command: drag from the Effects list onto a clip; right-click a clip, Effects; Effects menu, Apply to selected clip or Apply to whole video; double-click the effect (whole video). A single click opens the popup, titled "— <clip name>" or "— whole video" (Change aspect only).
- Drag inside the window uses egui's built-in support (no crate); a clip under the pointer highlights; elsewhere the drop is refused. It works on Wayland.
- **Undo:** a fade or subtitles is one step; Volume records a step only when a value changes (slider released, mute switched).
- **Whole video = copies (1.6.1, REQ-082, 147, 148; built 2.2.0):** a double-click applies the effect to every video clip at that moment, with the same result as on that clip. Each copy belongs to its clip: deleting the clip removes it, a clip added later has none, a clip that has the effect gets no second copy. Fade IN/OUT: fade in at the first clip's start, fade out at the last clip's end. Subtitles: per clip (REQ-085). Volume: each clip set to the value. Clean audio: on for each clip. Scale and Move: a copy of the keyframes in each clip. Apply LUT: the same file on each clip. Change aspect stays one setting of the video.

## Fade IN/OUT
- Named **Fade IN/OUT** in the interface since 1.3.0 (internal name and format unchanged). Text items have their own Fade IN and OUT ([text](architecture-text.md)).
- On a clip: fades at both edges. A boundary with a neighbour gets fade-out plus fade-in; the first clip fades in from black at the start, the last one out to black at the end. Each fade (start, end, each boundary) has its own marker and can be removed alone; the "+" handle at boundaries without a fade stays. Removing the effect removes both edge fades.
- Length (2.0.4, REQ-274, 275, ADR-190): 0.1 s up to half of the shorter clip; 0 means the default 0.5 s (half the clip under 1 s). Picture and sound together; a 1.5 s fade lasts 1.5 s within one frame (gate).
- Start and end fades are two flags of the clip (`fade_in`, `fade_out`; format 9) and move and are removed with it. Projects from 1.1 and earlier look as before.

## Volume, Clean audio and speed
- **Volume:** each video clip has its own level 0 to 100 % (no boost) and a mute switch; the video level multiplies it. Gain = (level / 100)^2 (50 % about −12 dB; muted is silence). A clip at 100 % gets no filter, so projects without clip volume give the 1.1 command (tested). A level other than 100 % adds a `volume` filter in the clip's audio chain before its fades (`volume=0.250000` at 50 %). Split gives both halves the level; copy, paste and reorder keep it; merge needs equal levels ("The clips have different volumes"). A marker at the clip start opens the popup.
- **Volume keys (2.0.4, REQ-280, 282):** `Clip.volume_keys` (`Key<u8>`, percent, at most 200), Straight or Ease per segment; gain (percent / 100)^2 through the expression generator. 0 % at 0 s, 100 % at 4 s, 50 % at 2 s gives gain 0.25 (gate). Music clips have their own volume, mute and Clean audio (REQ-276, 277).
- **Clean audio (1.4.0, REQ-114; strength REQ-278; ADR-080, 081):** FFmpeg `afftdn` (spectral noise reduction, in every bundled build, no model file); its three values are constants tuned once against the gate. Rejected: `anlmdn` (heavier), `arnndn` (model file), `agate` and `highpass` (do not remove noise under speech). Strength: dry and wet mix 0 to 100 % (0 bypasses). **Enhance speech (REQ-279):** `speechnorm`, named "Normalise speech" (`NORMALISE_SPEECH`). Position: the clip's audio chain before the clip volume and the fades; the music track is unchanged. Split keeps it; merge needs the same setting ("The clips have different clean audio settings"). Any clip with it forces a re-encode (REQ-023). Acceptance on a committed speech-with-noise clip: noise −6 dB, speech within 3 dB (assumptions).
- **Change speed (2.0.4 REQ-281; 2.3.0 as an effect):** `Clip.speed` in thousandths (1000 = normal), 50 % to 200 %. Timeline length = source length / speed (`Clip::len`; `src_len` the source part); keys and subtitle times stay in source time and are mapped (`to_timeline`, `to_src`). Picture `setpts`, sound `atempo`. Never stream copied; clips of different speeds do not merge (`DIFFERENT_SPEEDS`). 2.3.0: `EffectKind::ChangeSpeed`, `Instance::Speed(FrameTarget)`, `SetSpeedAll` (double-click: a copy on each clip, one undo step), not keyframed; overlaps are rescaled.
- **Crop removed (2.3.0, REQ-283):** no interface row, no writer; `Clip.crop` is read only (`skip_serializing`); format 13 crops are dropped on conversion with a count.

## Applied effects column and order (2.1.0 to 2.2.0, REQ-302 to 305, 311, 313, 317, 320 to 322)
- **Column (REQ-317, built 2.2.0):** right of the Preview section, beside the picture; width 15 % of the section, never under 180 px (at 1280x800 it is 180 px). Share in settings (`applied_share`, default 150, range 80 to 300; version stays 2). The column scrolls; popups float.
- **Content:** the effects of the selected clip (or the one under the playhead). Rows are rebuilt only when the clip set, the order or the effects change (revision counter, ADR-216; 0 rebuilds over 60 frames without change). Click opens the popup; movable rows move by drag, Move up and Move down, or keyboard; each move is one undo step ("Move LUT down", "Move effect" for a drag). Empty: "Select a clip to see its effects".
- **Groups (2.2.0, ADR-223, 229):** picture group, applied first to last: Crop (removed in 2.3.0), Change aspect, Apply LUT, text items, picture items, subtitle lines, Fade IN/OUT, Scale, Move (top drawn last). Sound group: Clean audio, Volume. Speed is fixed first and not stored. A row moves only inside its group.
- **Default order** = the 2.1.0 build's order: such a clip is built exactly as before (golden argv). Only a clip with another order stores its full order (`clip_order`, `ClipOrder { clip, rows }`, written when not empty; format 12 the movable rows, format 13 every row). A 2.1.0 project with changed movable rows opens with those rows on top of the default for the others.
- **Legacy order (files up to 2.0.4):** from the bottom: the LUT (under the texts), picture items, subtitle lines, then flat texts in item order.
- **New rows:** a new LUT, text, picture item or overlap goes on top of the other movable rows (`put_on_top`); a new Frame, Scale, Move, Fade, Volume, Clean audio or Crop row takes its **default place** (deviation from REQ-321: a Scale above Change aspect would scale the background). A copy takes the top of its group on its clip; later whole-video changes do not reorder copies. The 2.1.0 whole-video mark is removed (REQ-303 amended).
- **360 clips:** sphere-fixed texts and lines keep fixed rows (burned before the projection); the whole-sphere export ignores the order.
- **Model:** `src/effect_order.rs` (pure): `Row` (`Lut`, `Text(id)`, `Picture(id)`, `Overlap(id)`, `Subtitles`, `Volume`, `Clean`, `Frame`, `Scale`, `Move`, `Fade`, `Crop`, `Unknown`), `Present`, `legacy`, `resolve`, `to_store` (nothing for the default), `is_changed`, `moved`, `dragged`. `order_chain.rs`: the sound group (`DEFAULT_SOUND`, `resolve_sound`, `sound_swapped`, `with_sound`, `with_picture`, `moved_sound`, `expand_2_1_0`). `project/order.rs`: `clip_rows`, `order_of`, `changed_orders`, `set_order`, `put_on_top`, `repair_orders` (note "A damaged effect order was repaired"). `app/applied.rs`: `applied()` cache, `Command::MoveRow { clip, row, mv }`, `RowMove::{Up, Down, To}`. `ui/applied_effects.rs`: the column.
- **Three builds** chosen by the resolved order: default → legacy chain (golden argv, nothing stored); a 2.1.0 order (movable rows above the rest) → the overlay stage; any other → the step builder.
- **Overlay stage (`render/overlay.rs`, 2.1.0):** `attach` gives changed clips their `OverlayStep::{Lut, Ass, Pic}` bottom to top; `graph.rs overlay_chain` appends them after the fades (LUT `format=gbrp,lut3d=…`; text `subtitles=f=ov<clip>-t<id>.ass`; lines `ov<clip>-s.ass`, in clip time); the changed clips leave the global document; `FrameKey.order`.
- **Step builder (`render/steps.rs`, ADR-224, 225):** the stream is a *source*, a *layer* (fitted to the frame on a transparent RGBA surround: LUT and Fade via `format=gbrap`; Scale and Move on a transparent canvas so their order matters; a text drawn by a **matte** (the document on black and white: coverage `255 - (white - black)`, colours `black * 255 / covered`, `alphamerge`)) or a *frame* (over the background at the Change aspect row; `blur_chain`). Texts use `doc_shift` = clip start. Sound: `Segment.volume_first` orders the two filters; a swap never uses the overlay stage; the dry part of Clean audio takes its input at the same point. Audio fades stay after the sound group. Preview and export use the same builder.
- **Known limits:** a LUT in a changed-order clip grades the whole frame, bars and fades included. Tests: `tests/ffmpeg_integration/v21.rs`, `v22order.rs`; golden argv for formats 4, 5 and 12.

## Project formats (effects)
8 (1.6.0): LUT per clip and for the video. 9 (1.6.1): fades as clip flags; whole-video Scale, Move and LUT removed. 12 (2.1.0): `clip_order`, text shadow; a file opens with the legacy order. 13 (2.2.0, ADR-222): the full row list, text maximum; 12 read with defaults. 14 (2.3.0, ADR-230): overlaps; 13 converted in memory. Newer formats are refused unchanged (REQ-029). Details: [projects](architecture-projects.md).

## Tests
- Timeline: fades on the first, middle and last clips; whole-video copies; deleting one clip removes only its effects; a clip appended later has none; short-clip fade length; split, copy, merge and remove of volume, clean audio and speed.
- Export: `volume=0.250000` at 50 %; equal clip and video levels; a muted clip silent; re-encode forced; clean audio before the clip volume; with real ffmpeg, first and last frame black with a whole-video fade; a muted clip silent in the export only.
- Interface: a drop on clip 2 equals the right-click menu; a drop on the music track changes nothing; one undo restores; Volume 50 % sets three clips with one undo.
- Order: default argv identical; pairs of swaps differ where required; preview equals export; a swap of Volume and Clean audio changes the sound; model moves, insert on top, repair of unknown ids.

## Risks
Listed in [risks](architecture-risks.md#technical-risks): legacy stacking must stay byte-identical (gate); one filter step per row adds graph size; Clean audio is level normalisation only, not a neural model.
