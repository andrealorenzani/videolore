# Business — 360 video (2.0.4)

Back to the [index](business.md). Batch 14 of the [roadmap tasks](roadmap_tasks.md), built in the 2.0.4 development. It extends [Boxes on the picture and 360 video](business-picture.md) (REQ-100 to REQ-105, REQ-112). Decided by Andrea on 2026-11-20: the 360 export always prioritises speed. Stereo 3D, cube maps and 180° video stay out of scope. REQ-257 to REQ-260, REQ-268 and REQ-273 are not built yet and stay `(planned)`, on the roadmap.

## Subtitles and texts on the sphere
- REQ-257 (planned): A subtitle line on a 360 clip is drawn with the style's preset box and word highlight, as on a flat clip, in the preview and in the export. Amends REQ-112 and the limitation of 1.3.0 (plain white text). Acceptance: a line placed on a 360 clip shows its box and highlight in the preview and in the 360 export at three sample times.
- REQ-258 (planned): Subtitle lines generated automatically on a 360 clip are anchored to a point of the sphere, like the lines added by hand (REQ-112), so they stay in place when the camera turns. The anchor is the centre of the view at the time of generation. Amends the view-fixed default of REQ-112. Acceptance: generate on a 360 clip with the view turned to yaw 30°: after the camera turns to 0° the lines are 30° to the side; in the 360 export they keep yaw 30° (±1°).
- REQ-259 (planned): **Re-anchor** (Edit menu and the Context Menu, for the selected subtitle lines and text items) moves their anchor to the centre of the current view. Acceptance: re-anchor with the view at yaw 90° moves the item to yaw 90° (±1°) in the preview and in the export; one undo restores the old anchor.
- REQ-260 (planned): An item of a 360 clip whose point is outside the view shows a small arrow at the nearest picture edge, pointing to it. Clicking the arrow turns the view to the item. Acceptance: an item at yaw 90° while the view looks at 0° shows an arrow at the right edge; a click turns the view to yaw 90° (±1°).

## Export and files
- REQ-261 (implemented): A 360 video exported to Matroska (MKV) or WebM carries the spherical-video information that 360 players read, as MP4 and MOV do (REQ-103). The marker is written by the project's own Matroska writer when the bundled ffmpeg does not write it (decided by a spike in the build; if the spike fails, this requirement moves back to the roadmap and the export keeps no marker, as now). Amends the limitation of 1.3.0. Acceptance: ffprobe of the MKV and WebM output reports the equirectangular projection; a 360 player check is in [verification](business-verification.md).
- REQ-262 (implemented): A 360 video export keeps the spherical information of its source (projection and its values), so the output shows as the same sphere. Acceptance: the spherical fields of the output equal those of the source (test); the player check is in [verification](business-verification.md).
- REQ-263 (implemented): A clip whose file has no spherical information can be marked **360° video** in Clip details. The mark is stored in the project (project format 11, see the [editing](business-editing.md) document) and makes the clip a camera view and offers the 360 choice of the Export dialog. Amends REQ-100 and REQ-078 (the 1.4.0 switch was removed because flat pictures were shown as 360, so the mark is explicit). Acceptance: a plain 2:1 file marked as 360 shows the camera view; unmarked, it stays flat; the mark survives save and reopen; a 1.3.0 switch stays dropped (REQ-123).
- REQ-264 (implemented): With the 360 choice of the Export dialog, the presets are greyed out with the reason "A 360 video keeps the sphere's own size". Amends REQ-120. Acceptance: the choice shows the reason on the presets; the flat camera view still offers them.
- REQ-265 (implemented): With the 360 choice, a project that has Change aspect, Scale or Move shows the note "Frame effects are not applied to a 360 video; the flat camera view applies them." Amends REQ-131. Acceptance: the note appears with those effects and not without; the 360 output is the same with and without them.
- REQ-266 (implemented): A 360 export always uses the speed-first settings: the fast resampling of the reprojection and the fastest encoder preset of the chosen codec whose file stays within 1.5 times the Best quality size of the same settings (measured on the reference clip). A moving-camera export sends at most 10 camera updates per second of video to ffmpeg (about 30 today). The output keeps the same length, size and format. Amends REQ-103 (the speed requirement that REQ-103 lacked). Acceptance: the generated commands use the speed-first settings (test); the chosen preset's file is within 1.5 times the Best quality size on the reference clip (measured); a 10-second moving-camera export gets at most 100 updates.

## Camera
- REQ-267 (implemented): While the camera is dragged, the view is drawn at half resolution; at release the full-resolution view is drawn within 200 ms. Amends REQ-101 and REQ-104. Acceptance: during a drag the drawn view is half size (test); after release the full size is drawn within 200 ms.
- REQ-268 (planned): Each clip has its own camera path. A copy or a pasted clip gets a copy of the path, which then changes alone. Amends REQ-102 (copies keep their POVs and then diverge). Acceptance: copy a clip with two POVs, add a POV on the copy: the original's path is unchanged.
- REQ-269 (implemented): A 360 file's initial view (its starting heading, pitch and roll, when written in the file) is the starting view of the camera when the clip has no POV. Amends the limitation of 1.2.6. Acceptance: a fixture with an initial heading of 90° starts the camera at yaw 90° (±1°) before its first POV.
- REQ-270 (implemented): The camera has roll. **Shift+drag** on the picture rolls the view; POVs store the roll and the path interpolates it; Reset view sets roll to 0. Amends REQ-101 (no roll before). Acceptance: a POV with roll 30° at 5 s gives that roll at 5 s; a straight line in the scene stays straight in the view; Reset view returns to roll 0.
- REQ-271 (implemented): Each POV segment has a timing: **Straight** (the default, as now) or **Ease in and out**, chosen in the POV popup. Amends REQ-102 (the straight line stays the default). Acceptance: with ease in and out, the view at the middle of a segment is at the middle angle and at a quarter of the segment it is behind the straight path; both ends are exact.
- REQ-272 (implemented): When the preview has keyboard focus, the arrow keys turn the camera by 5 degrees, Page Up and Page Down zoom by 5 degrees, and Enter sets a POV at the playhead (REQ-102). Acceptance: UI test with the preview focused: Right turns the view by 5°, Enter adds a POV at the playhead; with other focus nothing changes.
- REQ-273 (planned): For a 360 clip with first-order ambisonic sound (ACN, SN3D), the sound follows the camera: videolore rotates it by the camera's yaw and pitch in Rust and reduces it to stereo with a fixed pair of cardioid virtual microphones, in the preview and in the flat camera view export. No extra data file is needed. The 360 video export keeps the sound as recorded (the viewer's player turns it). If the rotation cannot run in real time, the requirement moves back to the roadmap. Amends REQ-101 and the limitation of 1.2.6. Acceptance: a fixture with a sound on the left, camera turned 90° to the left: the sound is in front (channel levels within 3 dB, test); a sound check by ear is in [verification](business-verification.md).

## Key decisions (2.0.4)
- [MINOR] The frame effects are not applied to a 360 video export; the note of REQ-265 says so, and the flat camera view applies them. Reason: the sphere is written as recorded (REQ-103).
- [MINOR] The 360 video keeps the sphere's size and has no preset (REQ-264); a 360 preset is not needed.
- [MINOR] Automatic subtitle lines are anchored at the centre of the view when they are generated (REQ-258).
- [MINOR] Off-view arrows are shown for texts and lines only, not for POVs.
- [MINOR] REQ-273 is built only if the rotation runs in the preview at real time; otherwise it moves to the roadmap (Future work).

## Out of scope (2.0.4)
- Stereo 3D, cube maps, 180° video, headsets and motion sensors.
- The GPU or SIMD path for the reprojection: only if the look-around target of REQ-104 is missed; measured first (quality goals).
