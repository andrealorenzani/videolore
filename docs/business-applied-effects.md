# Business — Applied effects and their order (2.1.0)

Back to the [index](business.md). Built in 2.1.0, with [Subtitle and text styles](business-subtitle-styles.md) and the LUT import in [Apply LUT](business-lut.md). The current rules it changes are in [Effects](business-effects.md), [Text](business-text.md) and [Frame](business-frame.md).

Before 2.1.0 the picture was built in a fixed order: frame and Scale, Move, Change aspect, then Apply LUT, then the text items and subtitle lines on top. Nothing let the user choose the order. This release adds a list that shows what is applied to the selected clip and lets the user choose where a LUT or a text sits in the order.

## Features
- **Applied effects widget**: a new part of the preview, about 15% of its space, that lists the effects of the selected clip.
- **Order**: the top row is applied last (drawn on top); the lower rows are applied first (drawn underneath).
- **Reorder**: a LUT, a text item, a picture item or the subtitle lines of the clip can be moved up or down, so a LUT can colour a text or leave it alone.
- **Whole-video effects** (2.2.0): a whole-video effect is a copy on each clip (on the first and last clip for Fade IN/OUT; REQ-147), and the widget lists only the clip's own effects, with no mark (REQ-303, amended). The whole-video mark of 2.1.0 is removed.

## Requirements

### Applied effects widget
- REQ-302 (implemented): Since 2.2.0 the widget is a column on the right (REQ-317), and its rows follow REQ-320. The preview has an **Applied effects** widget of about 15% of the preview space (2.1.0 wording: a strip below the picture, the picture shrinks first, REQ-068). It lists the effects of the selected clip, or of the clip at the playhead when no clip is selected (assumption). Rows, from the top: the movable rows in the order of REQ-304 (Apply LUT, each text item and picture item that overlaps the clip in time, the clip's subtitle lines as one row "Subtitles"), then the fixed rows in the order the picture is built: Crop, Scale, Move, Change aspect, Fade IN/OUT, Volume, Clean audio, Speed (assumption: fixed rows sit below the movable ones, because the picture is built before them). Each row shows its icon, its name and, for texts, the time range. A click on a row opens its popup. With no clip, the widget says "Select a clip to see its effects". The widget scrolls when its rows do not fit. Acceptance: on a clip with Apply LUT, then one text added: the text row is above the LUT row; a clip with no effects shows only the empty text; a click on the LUT row opens the Apply LUT popup; at 800x500 with the Preview section at its minimum every row is reachable by the scroll.

- REQ-303 (implemented): The widget lists the effects of the clip only, with no whole-video mark: a whole-video effect is a copy on each clip (REQ-147), and each copy is an ordinary clip row. The 2.1.0 inferred mark (two or more clips with equal settings, the project's Change aspect, first Fade IN, last Fade OUT) is removed. Acceptance: after a double-click of Volume on clips 1 to 3, each clip's widget shows its Volume row without any mark; a change of the Volume on clip 2 leaves the rows of clips 1 and 3 unchanged; no row in the widget has a whole-video mark.

### Order and reorder
- REQ-304 (implemented): Since 2.2.0 all effects are movable (REQ-320). The order of the movable rows is chosen by the user in the widget: with **Move up** and **Move down** buttons, by drag, or with Alt+Up and Alt+Down while a row has the focus or the pointer. The order decides the picture: a row higher up is applied later and drawn on top. The movable rows are Apply LUT, each text item, each picture item and the subtitle lines of the clip. The fixed rows (Crop, Scale, Move, Change aspect, Fade IN/OUT, Volume, Clean audio, Speed) keep their place and cannot be moved. The order belongs to the clip: a text that overlaps two clips can be above the LUT in one and below it in the other. A new LUT or picture item is put on top once the edit is done, so a LUT applied to a clip with texts grades the texts; a new text is on top too, as the later item. Each move is one undo step named after it (REQ-074, for example "Undo Move LUT down"; a drag is "Move effect"). Acceptance: a text above a LUT on a clip shows the text in its own colours in the preview and in the export; the same text moved below the LUT shows the LUT colours; one undo restores the order; a text overlapping clips 1 and 2 with the LUT only on clip 2 gets the order of clip 2 there and no change on clip 1.

- REQ-305 (implemented): Texts overlapping on the picture: the lower row is covered by the higher row where the two boxes overlap, in the preview and in the export. Moving a row swaps which text covers which. Projects saved before 2.1.0 keep the drawing order they have today (a later text is drawn on top of an earlier one; accepted by Andrea in 2.2.0), so their picture does not change. Acceptance: two texts overlapping at the same time, A above B: A covers B in the preview and in the export; after Move down on A, B covers A in both; a project saved in 2.0.4 with overlapping texts exports the same picture as before.

### Project file
- REQ-311 (implemented): Since 2.2.0 the order of all effects is stored (REQ-322). The project stores, for each clip, the order of its movable rows (REQ-304), and the new settings of REQ-306 to REQ-310 (shadows, highlight colours, the Enlarge word size, the new styles). This is project format 12. Projects saved by 2.0.4 or earlier open in 2.1.0 with the same look and export (the defaults of the edge cases). A 2.1.0 project is refused by 2.0.4 and older with their newer-version message, and the file is left untouched (REQ-029). A damaged order entry is dropped with the line "A damaged effect order was repaired" in the "Some files could not be found or were repaired" notice, without a crash (REQ-057). Acceptance: a 2.0.4 example project opens and exports as before; a 2.1.0 file with a moved row is refused by 2.0.4 with its message; a damaged order entry is dropped with that line in the notice, without a crash.

## Edge cases and rules for the builders
- **Old projects**: a project saved before 2.1.0 has no stored order. It opens with the order that the picture has today (LUT below the texts and subtitle lines; texts in their present stacking), so its look and export do not change (REQ-311).
- **Render order**: the fixed rows keep the order of the render graph. Only the place of the movable rows relative to each other and to the picture layers changes. A LUT is always applied after Scale, Move and Change aspect, as today.
- **Export and preview parity**: both use the same order; the export is always a re-encode when the order differs from the default (REQ-023).
- **360 clips**: the order applies to anchored texts and anchored subtitle lines (REQ-257) and to the whole sphere picture in the same way as on a flat clip; those anchored items are fixed rows (see Known limitations). A 360 video export (the whole sphere) ignores the order; the flat camera view export keeps it.
- **Blink**: a hidden text (REQ-162) takes no place in the picture; its row stays in the widget.
- **Shadow and readability**: the order decides whether a LUT colours a text's shadow and outline too; the shadow of a text is part of the text (REQ-306).
- **Undo**: each reorder, and each change of a row's place in the list, is one undo step; the widget itself does not change the project.
- **Project file**: the order is stored in the project per clip (REQ-311).

## Layout and the order of all effects (2.2.0)
- REQ-317 (implemented): Amends REQ-302. The Applied effects widget is a **vertical column on the right side of the Preview section**, beside the picture, not a strip below it. Its width is about 15% of the Preview section's width, and never less than 180 px (assumption: an icon, a name and a time range fit). The 15% share applies from a section of 1200 px; at 1280 x 800 the column is 180 px (about 20%) (built 2.2.0). Its rows scroll vertically when they do not fit. The picture takes the rest of the section and keeps its aspect ratio; when the section is narrower than the picture's minimum plus the column's minimum, the picture shrinks first (REQ-068) and the column keeps its minimum width. The column is part of the Preview section: its width follows the section's border like the other section sizes of the layout, and it is remembered in the saved layout. Popups are floating windows and are not placed inside the column; they keep their remembered places. Acceptance: at 1280 x 800 the picture and the column are side by side and the column is about 15% of the section's width; at the minimum window size the column is 180 px and the picture takes the rest; a list of 20 rows scrolls inside the column; a dragged border changes the width and it is the same after a restart.
- REQ-320 (implemented; amended 2.3.0: Crop removed, Overlap media is a picture row, Change speed is the fixed row): Amends REQ-302 and REQ-304. Every effect of a clip can be reordered in the widget, not only Apply LUT, texts, picture items and subtitle lines. The widget shows one list with two groups and one fixed row:
  - **Picture group** (top row applied last, drawn on top): Change aspect (the clip's frame), Apply LUT, texts, picture items, Overlap media (REQ-325), subtitle lines, Fade IN/OUT, Scale, Move. Their order is the order of the picture build.
  - **Sound group**: Clean audio and Volume (REQ-280). Their order is the order of the sound build.
  - **Change speed**: a fixed row at the bottom, applied first (REQ-281); it sets the clip's time, so every other row is applied to the clip's new timing.
  A row moves only inside its group (a sound row cannot be placed among picture rows: the two chains do not act on each other, so such a position would have no meaning). The default order of an old project, and of a new clip, is the order the picture and the sound have today; the Architect writes that order into the golden tests, and an old project with no stored order keeps it (REQ-311). Examples of what the order decides: Move before Scale and after Scale give different places; a LUT above Fade IN/OUT keeps the fade black (black through the LUT stays black only when the LUT maps black to black), a LUT below the fade grades it; Volume above Clean audio raises the level before the noise reduction, below it after. No order is refused: every order is drawn as shown, and the result is the one the order says. Fade IN/OUT is an ordinary movable picture row: its time is the clip's timeline time after Speed (Speed is applied first), so a fade in the picture group is the same length as in the clip's timeline. Decided (Andrea, 2.2.0): Speed is fixed and applied first; the LUT's place against Change aspect and Fade IN/OUT is the user's choice, and moving the LUT above Change aspect grades the frame fill (roadmap item "LUT order with the frame effect", closed as a user choice). Today's order is the default for old projects and new clips. Built in 2.2.0: the Fade row shows as "Fade IN/OUT" only when the clip has several fades, otherwise each fade keeps its own name; a text on a reordered clip is drawn through a matte, which is slower. Acceptance: moving Volume above Clean audio gives a different sound in the preview and the export (test); Change speed cannot be moved; a row cannot be dropped in the other group; the default order of a 2.1.0 project is unchanged in the export (golden test).
- REQ-321 (implemented; amended 2.3.0: Crop removed, overlap media on top): Undo, parity and copies. Each move is one undo step, named after the row as REQ-304 does ("Undo Move Volume up"; a drag is "Move effect"). The preview and the export use the same chain (REQ-131 parity). Placement: a new text, picture item, Overlap media or LUT goes on top of the other movable rows, as in 2.1.0; a new Change aspect, Scale, Move or Fade takes its default place in the picture group, not the top (on top, a Scale above Change aspect would scale the background); a new sound row keeps the default order Clean audio then Volume. A whole-video effect (REQ-147) puts a copy on each clip it applies to, and each copy takes the place its row has by default on that clip (the rule above); a later whole-video change does not reorder the copies, and each copy is then moved on its own clip. Fade IN/OUT copies of a whole video take their clip's place in the picture group. 360 clips: the texts and lines fixed to the sphere keep fixed rows (Q-3, 2.1.0), and the 360 video export (the whole sphere) ignores the order; the flat camera view keeps it. Acceptance: moving a copy on clip 2 does not move the copy on clip 1; a new Scale takes its default place, not the top of the picture group; one undo restores the order; the preview and the export agree on a test frame.
- REQ-322 (implemented; amended 2.3.0): Amends REQ-311. The project stores the order of every movable row of each clip (REQ-320); Change speed is not stored in the order because it is fixed, its value is stored with the clip (REQ-281); a stored Crop row is dropped (REQ-332). A project saved before 2.2.0 has no stored order for the new rows and opens with the default order (REQ-320): its look and export do not change. The Architect decided project format 13 (ADR-222): 2.1.0 and older refuse it with their newer-version message and leave the file untouched (REQ-029); formats 12 and older still open. A damaged order entry is dropped with the 2.1.0 line "A damaged effect order was repaired" (REQ-311). Acceptance: a 2.1.0 example project opens and exports as before; a 2.2.0 project with Volume moved above Clean audio opens again in 2.2.0 with that order; the older program refuses it as REQ-029 says if the format changed.

### Decisions of the layout and the order (2.2.0)
- (Andrea 2026-11-21, GO) Every effect can be reordered inside its group (picture, sound); Speed is fixed. Reason: the user asked for all effects; the picture and the sound act on different chains, so their relative order has no meaning and is not offered.
- (Andrea, Q2) Speed is fixed and applied first; Fade IN/OUT stays movable in the picture group.
- (Andrea, Q3) Today's order is the default for old projects and new clips; the LUT can be moved above Change aspect, which grades the fill.
- [MINOR] The default order is the order of the build today, so no old output changes.
- [MINOR] Widget width 15% of the section, at least 180 px.
- (2.3.0, Andrea 2026-11-22) Crop is removed (REQ-283) and Speed is the fixed Change speed row (REQ-281); Volume is a keyframed effect in the Sound group (REQ-280); Overlap media is a picture row on top by default (REQ-325, REQ-331).

## Key decisions (2.1.0)
- [MINOR] The movable rows are Apply LUT, texts, picture items and the subtitle lines; the other effects keep their place, because moving them would change the picture build. Reason: the user asked for the LUT and the texts; the other order choices are not asked for and would change many existing outputs.
- [MINOR] The order is per clip, not per timeline: one text can have a different order on each clip it overlaps. Reason: the widget shows one clip, and a whole-video LUT is a copy on each clip (REQ-147).
- [MINOR] A new text or LUT is applied last (top). Reason: the user adds an effect and then decides whether an earlier one is applied to it.
- [MINOR] The rows follow the user's list, and the list is the "order of application" the user asked for; the fixed rows are shown below the movable rows because they are applied first.

## Key decisions (2.2.0)
- (Andrea 2026-11-21) Whole-video effects are distributed as copies and the whole-video mark is removed (REQ-303, REQ-147). Reason: the whole video was meant to be applied to all clips and then behave as a clip effect. The mark was asked for in the 2.1.0 request; this removes it.
- (Andrea 2026-11-21) REQ-305 is accepted as built: old projects keep their stacking, and a new LUT or picture item goes on top.

## Known limitations
- Overlapping subtitle lines (REQ-169) are not ordered by the widget; subtitle lines of one clip stay in their own order.
- Texts that overlap several clips can have a different order on each clip; the widget shows the order of the selected clip only.
- The texts and lines fixed to the sphere of a 360 clip keep fixed rows; they cannot be moved in the order (decision Q-3, 2.1.0).
- A 360 video export (the whole sphere) ignores the order.
- The subtitle lines that go to a separate subtitle track are not drawn by the clip, so the order does not apply to them.

## Out of scope (2.1.0)
- (amended in 2.2.0) Reordering fades, volume, Clean audio, crop, Scale, Move and Change aspect is now in scope (REQ-320); Speed stays fixed (REQ-320).
- Ordering of individual subtitle lines in the widget; layers on the timeline (multiple video tracks stay out of scope).
- Keyframes for the order, and a separate order for each word.
