# Business — New effects, frame and LUT options (2.0.4, changed 2.3.0)

Back to the [index](business.md). Each effect has its own requirements here. The existing documents ([Effects](business-effects.md), [Frame](business-frame.md), [Apply LUT](business-lut.md), [Music and volume](business-music.md)) point here where it matters.

## Fades, music and sound
- REQ-274 (implemented): The length of each fade is chosen in its popup, from 0.1 s to half of the shorter of the two clips. The default is 0.5 s, or half of a shorter clip. Amends REQ-011 and REQ-083 (the fixed length of decision D1). Acceptance: a 1.5 s fade lasts 1.5 s (within one frame) in the preview and in the export; a fade longer than half of a 2 s clip stops at 1 s; one undo restores the old length.
- REQ-275 (implemented): A fade at a boundary can be **both**, **out only** (the fade to black before the boundary) or **in only** (the fade from black after it). Amends REQ-011. Acceptance: an out-only fade darkens only the clip before the boundary; an in-only fade darkens only the clip after it, in the preview and in the export.
- REQ-276 (implemented): A music clip has its own volume (slider and mute, as a video clip's, REQ-084), and Fade IN/OUT can be applied to it by drag or right-click. Amends REQ-082. The music track volume (REQ-020) and the rule that music does not ripple (REQ-043) stay. Acceptance: a music clip at 50% is 50% lower in the preview and export; a fade on a music clip is audible at its start and end; the music track is unchanged.
- REQ-277 (implemented): **Clean audio** can be applied to a music clip (drag or right-click) and to the music track as a whole. Amends REQ-114. Acceptance: a music clip with Clean audio gives a different sound than without (test of the filter graph); the export and the preview use the same filters; a listening check is in [verification](business-verification.md).
- REQ-278 (implemented): The Clean audio popup has a **Strength** slider from 0% to 100%, default 100% (the 1.4.0 values). 0% leaves the sound as it is. Stored in the project. Acceptance: at 0% the output equals the input within 0.1 dB; at 100% it equals the 1.4.0 output; a value in between gives a noise reduction between the two.
- REQ-279 (implemented): The Clean audio popup has an **Enhance speech** switch, off by default, that adds speech normalisation after noise reduction: the ffmpeg `speechnorm` filter, named "Normalise speech" in the popup. No speech model file is bundled (Andrea, 2026-11-21). Acceptance: with the switch on the export filter graph contains `speechnorm` (test); the result by ear is in [verification](business-verification.md).
- REQ-280 (implemented): Volume is keyframed (2.3.0). **Volume** is an effect of a clip (drag, right-click or Effects list, like Scale and Move). Its popup has the keyframe timeline of REQ-129 with Add keyframe (at the playhead), Remove keyframe and Remove effect; each keyframe has its own straight or ease choice (REQ-282); a clip has at most one Volume effect. Each keyframe holds the clip's volume in percent (0% to 100%); between two keyframes the percent changes in a straight line, or eased; the gain is (percent / 100) squared. One keyframe means a constant volume. A new Volume effect starts with one keyframe at the clip's current volume (MINOR). The clip volume slider and mute (REQ-084) stay in Clip details; while a Volume effect is set, the slider shows that the keyframes set the volume. Volume keys stored by 2.2.0 become the clip's Volume effect (REQ-332). Amends REQ-020 and REQ-084. Acceptance: 0% at 0 s and 100% at 4 s gives 50% at 2 s, a gain of 0.25; one keyframe gives the same volume everywhere; a clip without the effect keeps its slider level; the preview and the export agree; Clip details has no volume keys row.

## Clip timing and picture
- REQ-281 (implemented): Speed is the effect **Change speed** (2.3.0; not a Clip details row): a value from 50% to 200%, not keyframed. It is applied to one video clip (drag or right-click) or to all clips (double-click, one copy on each clip as REQ-147 gives). It is not in Change aspect. Effect as built in 2.0.4: the clip plays faster or slower, its sound keeps its pitch, and its timeline length changes (the timeline ripples); subtitle lines stay with their speech (REQ-018), mapped to the new times; Volume keyframes (REQ-280) stay in timeline time; overlaps of the clip (REQ-325) are rescaled with its times (REQ-329); a clip with a speed is never stream copied; a merge needs equal speeds. Its row in the Applied effects widget is the fixed row applied first (REQ-320). Speeds stored by 2.2.0 are kept (REQ-332). Acceptance: a 4 s clip at 200% occupies 2 s; the preview and export show the same length and picture; a subtitle line stays within 0.1 s of its word; the pitch is unchanged within 1% (test); one undo restores the value.
- REQ-282 (implemented): A keyframe segment of Scale, Move or Volume is **Straight** (the default) or **Ease in and out**, chosen at the keyframe that starts the segment. Amends REQ-129. Acceptance: an ease segment is at its middle value at its middle time and is behind the straight path at a quarter of its length; the first and last value are exact.
- REQ-283 (implemented; removed in 2.3.0): **Crop is removed** (Andrea, 2026-11-22). The Crop row of the Applied effects widget, the Crop % values of Clip details and the crop of the picture go. Old crops are dropped on opening with the notice of REQ-332.
- REQ-332 (implemented; new in 2.3.0): **Opening 2.2.0 projects.** Volume keyframes become the clip's Volume effect with the same keys (REQ-280); a clip speed other than 100% becomes the clip's Change speed with the same value (REQ-281); a crop is dropped (REQ-283) and the picture returns to full size. One opening status line reads "Opening: N clip(s) changed by the conversion (M crop(s) dropped, K picture(s) made overlaps)"; no line when nothing changed; no undo step and no change mark. Stored orders (REQ-322) drop the Crop row; Speed is not stored in the order. Older versions refuse the saved file and leave it untouched (REQ-029). Damaged values are corrected or dropped without a crash (REQ-057). Acceptance: a 2.2.0 project with a clip at 150%, volume keys 0% at 0 s and 100% at 4 s, and a 10% crop from the left opens with the clip at 150% as Change speed, the keys on its Volume effect, the picture uncropped, and one status line saying one crop was dropped.
- REQ-284 (implemented): The Change aspect popup has a **Blur** choice (Low, Medium, High). Medium is the strength of REQ-126 and is the default, so existing projects look the same. Stored in the project. Amends REQ-126. Acceptance: a project without the choice keeps Medium; High blurs more than Medium; the preview and the export agree.
- REQ-285 (implemented): The Change aspect **Aspect** list has **Custom…**, where the user types a width and a height (for example 1:1 or 21:9). A ratio must be between 1:4 and 4:1; other values are refused with a message. Amends REQ-124. Acceptance: 1:1 gives a square frame; 1:5 is refused; undo restores the previous shape.
- REQ-286 (implemented): A video clip can have its own Change aspect (its shape, fit and empty space). Its picture is composed in its own frame, which is fitted, centred and padded inside the project frame, whose size is the whole-video Change aspect's or the source's. The project frame is the only output size. Amends REQ-124 and REQ-130. Acceptance: clip 2 at 9:16 among 16:9 clips is shown as a portrait picture centred in the project frame with its sides empty; the output size is unchanged; the stream copy rule is not used for that clip.
- REQ-287 (implemented; amended 2.3.0): The **Add Picture** row and its Edit menu entry are replaced by **Overlap media** (REQ-325, REQ-331), see [Overlap media](business-overlap.md). The picture items of older projects are handled as REQ-330 decides. As built in 2.0.4 (kept for those items): a PNG with its transparency at the playhead, on its own lane like text items, 3 s by default; moved, scaled and aligned as a text item (REQ-097, REQ-098, REQ-117); its box is a preview aid; burned into the export at its place; shapes are not included; it takes no text effects; a 360 export does not draw it; a missing picture file stops an export. Acceptance (2.0.4): a PNG added at 2 s shows for 3 s; it moves and scales in the preview; the export has it at the same place within 2% of the frame; the transparent parts show the picture behind.

## Effects list and Change aspect
- REQ-288 (implemented): The Change aspect row can be dragged onto a video clip (it then applies to that clip, REQ-286); a double-click still applies it to the whole video. Amends REQ-075 and REQ-130. Acceptance: dragging the row onto clip 2 gives clip 2 a Change aspect; double-click gives the whole video one.

## LUT options
- REQ-289 (implemented): The Apply LUT popup has a **Strength** slider from 0% to 100%, default 100%. The picture is mixed with the LUT result by this amount. Amends REQ-135. Acceptance: 0% shows the source colours; 100% shows the full LUT output; 50% lies between them, within 2 colour levels of the average.
- REQ-290 (implemented): `.3dl` and `.csp` LUT files are read as well as `.cube`, with the same refusal rule for broken files (REQ-136). Acceptance: a valid fixture in each format gives the same colours as its `.cube` equivalent within 2 levels; a broken file is refused with its message and nothing changes.
- REQ-291 (implemented): A `.cube` file whose input range (DOMAIN_MIN and DOMAIN_MAX) is not 0 to 1 is read by scaling its input range to 0 to 1, when the minimum is below the maximum. Amends REQ-136. Acceptance: a fixture with a range of 0 to 2 gives the same output as the 0-to-1 version given the scaled input; a range with minimum not below maximum is refused.
- REQ-292 (implemented): When a LUT file is missing at export, the export stops before writing (REQ-136) and asks: **Find the LUT file…** (chooses the file and relinks the project), **Export without the LUT** (writes the file with a warning in the status line and the progress) or **Cancel** (writes nothing). Amends REQ-136. Acceptance: with a missing file the three choices appear; Export without the LUT writes a file with the warning and no LUT colours; Cancel writes nothing; Find relinks and the export then uses the LUT.
- REQ-293 (implemented): A project stores a copy of each LUT file it uses, inside the project file, so a moved or deleted file does not break it; the path stays for display and for Find (REQ-292). Limits (MINOR, ADR-191): a LUT above 20 MB is refused with a message, the copies of one project total at most 64 MB, and the project file is read up to 96 MiB. Amended by REQ-301 (2.1.0): above 20 MB the LUT is not refused but its path only is kept. Acceptance: delete the `.cube` after choosing it: the project opens and exports with the LUT; the project file grows by the LUT's size; a 21 MB LUT is refused.
- REQ-294 (implemented): **Favourite LUTs** is a list in the Apply LUT popup of up to 20 files kept in Settings (a program setting). A favourite is chosen with one click; a favourite whose file is missing is marked. Acceptance: add a favourite and it is listed after a restart; choosing it applies the file; a missing favourite shows the mark.

## Key decisions
- [MINOR] Per-clip Change aspect (REQ-286) is a clip's own frame placed inside the project frame; the output size stays one per project.
- (2.3.0, Andrea 2026-11-22) Crop is removed (REQ-283); old crops are dropped on opening with a notice (REQ-332). Speed is the effect Change speed, 50% to 200%, not keyframed (REQ-281); old speeds are kept (REQ-332).
- (2.3.0, Andrea 2026-11-22) Volume is keyframed as an effect of its own (REQ-280); the Volume keys row of Clip details is removed. Fades and text keyframes stay on the roadmap.
- [MINOR] A LUT colours the whole picture; the solid colour of the frame keeps its chosen colour (no change to REQ-131 or REQ-135).
- [MINOR] Picture items (REQ-287) are PNG images only; shapes stay on the roadmap.
- LUT colour management is not built: no product rule exists without a colour workflow (roadmap).

## Out of scope
- Colour management for LUTs; exact conversion of a combined Scale and Move (only if a user reports a changed old project).
- Keyframes for fades and text; shapes; stabilisation; transitions other than fades to black.
