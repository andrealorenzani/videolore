# Business — Effects

Back to the [index](business.md). The window and menus are in [Window, menus and effects](business-ui.md); Change aspect, Scale and Move in [Frame](business-frame.md); Apply LUT in [Apply LUT](business-lut.md); text effects in [Text](business-text.md).

An effect applied to the whole video (double-click) becomes a copy on each clip: on every clip for Volume, Scale, Move, Apply LUT and Clean audio, and on the first clip's start and the last clip's end for Fade IN/OUT (REQ-147, REQ-083). Each copy is then an ordinary clip effect. A whole-video Scale or Move gives each clip its own copy of the keyframes (REQ-148). Change aspect is the one exception: it sets the project frame.

## Requirements

### Effects
- REQ-075 (implemented): The Effects section is a vertical list: Fade IN/OUT, Subtitles, Volume, Clean audio (REQ-114), Change aspect, Scale, Move (REQ-130), Apply LUT (REQ-135), then "Add Text" (REQ-108). Each row shows its icon (REQ-142) and its name. The rows Fade IN/OUT, Subtitles, Volume, Clean audio, Scale, Move and Apply LUT have the tooltip "Drag onto a clip to apply it to that clip; double-click to apply it to the whole video"; "Change aspect" has "Sets the frame of the whole video"; "Add Text" has "Opens a popup to add a text at the playhead". Clicking an effect row opens its popup; clicking "Add Text" opens the text popup; double-clicking applies the effect to the whole video (REQ-082). The rows Fade IN/OUT, Subtitles, Volume, Clean audio, Scale, Move and Apply LUT can be dragged onto video clips; "Change aspect" and "Add Text" cannot. The list scrolls if needed. Acceptance: the row names are exact; clicking each opens its popup; the tooltips exist; "Add Text" and "Change aspect" cannot be dropped on a clip.
- REQ-107 (implemented): The effect "Fade through black" is named "Fade IN/OUT" everywhere the user sees it: the Effects list, the Effects and right-click menus, the popup title, tooltips and the shortcuts window. Its behaviour is unchanged (REQ-011, REQ-083). The text effects "Fade IN" and "Fade OUT" (REQ-109) are separate settings of a text item. Acceptance: no user-visible "Fade through black" remains; the effect works as before.
- REQ-082 (implemented): Each effect (Fade IN/OUT, Subtitles, Volume, Clean audio, Scale, Move, Apply LUT) can be applied **to a video clip**, by dragging it from the Effects list onto the clip or by right-clicking the clip and choosing Effects ▸ <effect>, or **to the whole video**, by double-clicking it in the Effects list; the whole video means the clips on the timeline at that moment (REQ-147). A single click opens the effect's popup (REQ-076). Each application opens the popup of the instance created or changed and is one undo step; for Volume the undo step is recorded when a value is changed in the popup (slider release or mute). Dropping an effect anywhere other than a video clip does nothing, and while dragging the pointer shows whether a drop is possible. Acceptance: (a) for each effect, dropping it on clip 2 of 3 gives the same result as the right-click menu, and one undo restores the previous state; (b) double-clicking gives the whole-video result (REQ-083, REQ-084, REQ-085; for Scale and Move the copies of REQ-148) and one undo restores the previous state; (c) dropping on the music track or on empty space changes nothing.
- REQ-147 (implemented): An effect applied to the whole video (double-click, REQ-082) is put on the video clips on the timeline at that moment as copies with the same settings: on every clip for Volume, Scale, Move, Apply LUT and Clean audio; for Fade IN/OUT only the first clip's start (fade in) and the last clip's end (fade out), as REQ-083 says. From then on each copy is an ordinary clip effect: it is shown in that clip's widget and popup with no whole-video mark, is changed, removed and undone on its clip only, and is deleted with its clip. A clip added later (appended, pasted, split off) gets none. A clip that already has the effect is not given a second copy. One double-click is one undo step. Exception: Change aspect is not copied: a double-click sets the project frame (REQ-124, REQ-286); whole-video Scale and Move give each clip its own copy of the keyframes (REQ-148). Acceptance: with clips 1 to 3, double-clicking Volume at 50% sets each clip to 50% and each popup shows its own value; a change on clip 2 alone leaves clips 1 and 3 unchanged; one undo restores all three; double-clicking Fade IN/OUT gives a fade in at clip 1's start and a fade out at clip 3's end, each changeable in its clip's popup; deleting clip 2 removes only its own copy; a clip appended afterwards has none; no clip shows a whole-video mark.
- REQ-076 (implemented): Effect popups are floating windows inside videolore (like Settings), not blocking: editing and playback continue while one is open. There is one per effect type; each can be moved and closed with its close button or Esc. Changes apply immediately, show in the preview and are each one undo step.
  - Fades: explains the effect (fixed length, REQ-011; video and audio); "Add fade at the boundary nearest the playhead"; list of all fades with their time (clicking one moves the playhead there and opens that fade's popup); "Remove all fades" (one undo step).
  - Subtitles: style, language, Generate / Cancel with progress and speech file download progress, "Add line at playhead", and a scrollable list of lines (clicking one opens that line's popup). REQ-014 to REQ-018, REQ-044 and REQ-052 to REQ-054 are unchanged.
  - Volume: video sound and music sliders and mute switches (REQ-020 unchanged; one undo step per slider release).
  - Acceptance: changing the subtitle style changes the preview and undo restores it; with 3 fades, "Remove all fades" leaves 0 and one undo brings back 3; playback continues while a popup is open.
- REQ-077 (implemented): Every fade and every subtitle line has a marker on the timeline at its position; so do the fade from black at the start of the video and the fade to black at its end (REQ-083), and every video clip whose own volume is not 100% or is muted (marker at the clip start, REQ-084). Markers differ by type (shape and colour); the tooltip gives the type, the time and, for subtitles, the start of the line's text. Clicking a marker selects that instance, moves the playhead to its start, highlights it and opens its popup:
  - Fade between clips: the two clips it sits between, its time, and Remove. Start or end fade: its time and Remove.
  - Clip volume: that clip's volume slider and mute switch; with a Volume effect (REQ-280) also its keyframes.
  - Subtitle line: a text field, start and end adjustable by ±0.1 s, Delete, "Play this line" and "Reset position" (REQ-089). Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on macOS) on a line marker adds it to or removes it from the selection instead (REQ-090).
  - A clip boundary without a fade shows a small "Add fade" handle; clicking it adds a fade (one undo step). Clicking an existing fade marker opens its popup; it does not remove the fade.
  - When several markers overlap under the pointer, a small chooser lists them all. Markers follow edits (ripple, REQ-018) and zoom.
  - Acceptance: (a) a marker's position equals its instance's start within 1 pixel at minimum, middle and maximum zoom; (b) clicking a fade marker opens the popup for that boundary, Remove makes the marker disappear and undo brings it back; (c) clicking a line marker opens the popup with that line's text, and editing it can be undone; (d) after cutting a clip its line markers are gone; (e) with overlapping markers the chooser lists all of them.

## Key decisions
- An effect applied to the whole video is distributed to the clips when applied and then behaves as a clip effect (Andrea, 2026-11-21). The whole-video mark of the Applied effects widget is removed. Change aspect stays the project frame (MINOR: one output size per project).
- Whole-video Fade IN/OUT keeps its place: the fade in at the first clip's start and the fade out at the last clip's end (REQ-083). Only the fades are copies that become ordinary clip fades; the copy on every clip applies to the other whole-video effects (Andrea, REFINE).
- Clips added after a whole-video effect do not get it (REQ-147); a note appears.
