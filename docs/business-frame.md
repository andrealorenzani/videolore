# Business — Frame: change aspect, scale, move and keyframes

Back to the [index](business.md).

Three picture effects. **Change aspect** sets the shape of the whole video (4:3, 16:9 or 9:16) and what fills the empty space; a clip has its own Change aspect too (REQ-326). **Scale** zooms the picture and **Move** shifts it. Scale and Move can be keyframed: their value changes in a straight line between keyframes.

## Features
- **Change aspect** (whole video; and per clip, REQ-326): choose the shape, then how the picture fits (**Fit on width** or **Fit on height**), then what fills the empty space (**Fit empty with colour**, default black, or **Fit empty with blurred video**). Example: a 1600x1200 video changed to 9:16 with Fit on width gives a 1600x2844 frame, the picture in the middle and about 822 px empty above and below. Fit on height gives a 676x1200 frame (675 rounded up to an even number), and the sides of the picture beyond it are cut evenly.
- **Scale**: a box around the picture with a corner handle at each corner; width and height change together.
- **Move**: a target at the centre of the picture; drag it to move the picture.
- **Keyframes** (Scale and Move): a small timeline in the popup, with **Add keyframe** and **Remove keyframe**. The value changes evenly between keyframes; before the first and after the last it stays the same.
- Scale and Move are applied to one clip (drag, or right-click > Effects) or to every clip (double-click: each clip gets its own copy, REQ-148). Change aspect of the whole video is one setting.

## Requirements

### Change aspect
- REQ-124 (implemented): **Change aspect** is an effect of the whole video; a project has at most one. Its popup has three dropdowns: **Aspect** (4:3, 16:9, 9:16); **Fit** (Fit on width, Fit on height); **Empty space** (Fit empty with colour, which shows a colour chooser, default black; or Fit empty with blurred video, REQ-126). A new effect starts with 16:9, Fit on width and black. The popup has a **Remove effect** button. Each change, and each removal, is one undo step. Acceptance: the dropdowns show exactly these options; a change shows in the preview at once; Remove effect gives back the source frame; one undo restores the previous choice.
- REQ-125 (implemented): The frame of Change aspect. The base size is the size the video would have without the effect (REQ-022, or the video preset of REQ-120). **Fit on width** keeps the base width and computes the height from the chosen shape. **Fit on height** keeps the base height and computes the width. The picture keeps its shape and is never stretched: on the side where it is longer than the frame, the extra part is cut equally from both sides; on the side where it is shorter, the empty space of REQ-126 is shown, centred. Frame sides are even numbers; a side above 4096 px is scaled down with the shape kept (assumption, see limitations). Preview and export have the same frame. Acceptance: a 1600x1200 video to 9:16 with Fit on width gives a 1600x2844 frame, the picture 1600x1200 centred, 822 px empty above and below (±1 px); the same with Fit on height gives a 676x1200 frame (675 rounded to an even number) with the picture 1600 px wide, 462 px cut from each side (±1 px); a video already in the chosen shape has no empty space.
- REQ-126 (implemented): **Fit empty with blurred video** fills the empty space with the same video, scaled to cover the whole frame and blurred with a fixed strength chosen by videolore (no setting). The sharp picture of REQ-125 is drawn on top, centred. A frame with no empty space shows no blur. **Fit empty with colour** fills it with the chosen colour (black by default). Acceptance: 1600x1200 to 9:16 with Fit on width and blur: the areas above and below show a blurred copy and no sharp edge is visible at the picture border; the sharp picture is unchanged against REQ-125 (±1 px); with colour the empty areas show exactly that colour.
- REQ-326 (implemented; amends REQ-286): The **Aspect of this clip** row of Clip details (REQ-286) offers the same choices as the whole-video Change aspect: its shape (with Off), **Fit** (Fit on width, Fit on height, REQ-125) and **Empty space** (Fit empty with colour, or Fit empty with blurred video with its blur level, REQ-126 and REQ-284). A clip's Change aspect that is first set starts as the whole video's does: Fit on width and black (MINOR). Off removes it. Each change is one undo step. Acceptance: on a 1600x1200 clip in a 16:9 project, Fit on width and Fit on height give the frames of REQ-125 for that clip (the picture kept whole, empty space shown); a vertical clip with Fit on width is cut at the top and bottom; a colour or blur choice shows in the empty space; undo restores the previous choice. The whole-video popup keeps its Fit and empty-space choices (REQ-124 to REQ-126) and is checked in the running popup.

### Scale and Move
- REQ-127 (implemented): **Scale** zooms the picture of a video clip or of the whole video (REQ-130). The preview shows a box around the picture with a square handle at each corner; dragging a corner scales the picture, width and height together, so the shape never changes; the opposite corner stays fixed. The size is a percentage of the picture's normal size (100% as it is), between 10% and 400% (assumption). Empty space that a smaller picture leaves shows the frame background (REQ-131). The popup shows the value in percent. Acceptance: dragging a corner outward enlarges the picture around the opposite corner; at 50% the frame shows the background around the picture; width and height keep their ratio at every step; 10% and 400% are the limits; one drag is one undo step (for a keyframe, see REQ-129).
- REQ-128 (implemented): **Move** shifts the picture of a video clip or of the whole video (REQ-130). The preview shows one round target at the centre of the picture; dragging it moves the picture with it. The centre of the picture may go anywhere on the frame but not past its edge, so the target can always be grabbed again (assumption, as REQ-117). The position is stored as a fraction of the frame, so it survives a change of frame size. Acceptance: dragging the target right by 10% of the frame width moves the picture by 10% (±1%) of the frame, in preview and export alike; dragging past the edge stops at the edge; one undo restores the place.
- REQ-129 (implemented): **Keyframes** of Scale and Move. When Scale or Move is added to a clip, a small popup opens (non-modal, REQ-076); a whole-video Scale or Move gives each clip its own copy, edited in its clip's popup (REQ-148). The popup shows a timeline of the clip's length, as wide as the popup allows, the keyframes as markers, and two buttons: **Add keyframe** adds one at the playhead with the value shown at that moment; **Remove keyframe** removes the selected keyframe. A new Scale or Move starts with one keyframe at the beginning (100% or the centre). Between two neighbouring keyframes the value changes in a straight line in time, with no easing. Before the first keyframe and after the last one the value stays the value of the nearest keyframe, so one keyframe means a constant value. A change on the picture (REQ-127, REQ-128) at the playhead changes the selected keyframe or, when none is selected, creates one at the playhead (assumption). Two keyframes cannot have the same time; the last remaining keyframe cannot be removed; the popup has a **Remove effect** button. A split, trim or cut of a clip keeps the keyframes as the points of view do (REQ-102): the value at each new edge stays the one the path had there, and keyframes outside the kept part are hidden, not lost. Each Add, Remove, value change and Remove effect is one undo step. A clip holds at most 200 keyframes per effect (assumption). Acceptance: Scale 100% at 0 s and 200% at 4 s gives 150% (±1%) at 2 s, 100% at 0 s and 200% from 4 s on; with one keyframe the value is the same everywhere; removing the first of two keyframes makes the second one's value apply from the start; Add at 1 s changes nothing on the picture at that moment; the timeline fills the popup width at any clip length; the popup stays open during playback.
- REQ-145 (implemented): The visible edges of the Scale box work as resize handles, as its corners do. The box is drawn around the picture; where it reaches beyond the frame (a side cut by Change aspect, or a Scale above 100%), the part of its edges inside the frame is the handle. Dragging an edge scales the picture proportionally, the opposite side staying fixed, so the ratio is kept at every step (REQ-127); the limits are 10% and 400%, and one drag is one undo step. The corner handles are drawn on the visible part of the picture, so a corner outside the frame is still reachable through its visible edge. This holds for a clip's Scale and for the whole video's Scale. Acceptance: with a 16:9 video in a 9:16 frame with Fit on height (sides cut), dragging a visible side of the frame changes the Scale and keeps the ratio; with Scale at 300% the visible edges still scale it back in one drag; one undo restores the previous value.
- REQ-148 (implemented): A whole-video Scale or Move (double-click, REQ-082, REQ-147) gives each video clip its own copy of the effect, with the keyframes of the whole-video Scale or Move as they are at that moment, in clip time (time 0 is the clip's start, REQ-129). Each copy is then a clip's own Scale or Move: it is edited in that clip's popup and on the picture, and changes that clip only. There is no whole-video popup: the popup that opens after a double-click is the one of the clip under the playhead (assumption), and each copy is removed with Remove effect in its own clip's popup. Deleting a clip deletes its copy; a clip added later gets none (REQ-147). A copy follows its clip through trims and cuts as a clip's own keyframes do (REQ-129). One double-click is one undo step for all copies. Acceptance: double-clicking Scale on clips 1 to 3 gives each clip one keyframe at 100%; changing clip 2's keyframe in its own popup changes only clip 2; removing clip 2's Scale leaves clips 1 and 3 unchanged; deleting clip 2 removes its copy and nothing else; a clip appended afterwards has no copy; one undo restores all three copies.

### Effects list, menus and markers
- REQ-130 (implemented): The Effects list (REQ-075) gets three rows, after Clean audio and before Add Text: **Change aspect**, **Scale**, **Move**. The Effects menu lists the same three (REQ-069). Scale and Move can be dragged onto a video clip, double-clicked for the whole video, and are in the clip's right-click Effects ▸ submenu (REQ-079, REQ-082). Change aspect is for the whole video only: it cannot be dropped on a clip and is not in the clip's submenu (the clip's own Change aspect is in Clip details, REQ-326). A clip with Scale or Move has one marker at its start (as Clean audio, REQ-114); the tooltip gives the effect and its keyframe count, and a click opens the clip's popup. A clip can have one Scale and one Move; adding another one opens the existing popup. A whole-video Scale or Move is one copy per clip (REQ-148), so each clip has at most one Scale and one Move. A 1.6.0 clip with its own Scale or Move and a whole-video one gets one combined path (REQ-149). Acceptance: the three row names are exact; dragging Scale onto clip 2 of 3 scales only that clip; dropping Change aspect on a clip changes nothing; the marker opens the popup; a second Scale on one clip opens the first one's popup.

### Picture rules and export
- REQ-131 (implemented): The picture is composed in this order, the same in preview and export: (1) the frame background: the colour or blurred copy of Change aspect, otherwise black; (2) the picture: the fit of REQ-125, then the clip's Scale and Move (a whole-video Scale or Move is a copy on each clip, REQ-148); (3) Fade IN/OUT (REQ-011) and the text effects; (4) subtitle lines and text items (REQ-089, REQ-111) at their places as fractions of the frame, so they do not move when the picture is scaled or moved (assumption). Any Change aspect, Scale or Move makes the export a re-encode (REQ-023) even at the default values; the hardware export and its output check apply (REQ-062, REQ-063). A 360 video export (REQ-103) keeps the whole sphere and ignores Change aspect, Scale and Move; the flat camera view applies them after the camera view. Acceptance: with Scale at 50% a subtitle line at the bottom of the frame stays at the bottom of the frame; preview and export match within 2% of the frame height; a project with Scale at 100% and no other change is re-encoded; a 360 video export is the same with and without these effects.

### Project file
- REQ-132 (implemented): A project stores the Change aspect settings, and each Scale and Move with its keyframes, for the whole video and for each clip. This is project format 1.5.0 (like REQ-123). A project saved with 1.5.0 cannot be opened by 1.4.1 or earlier, which refuse it with their newer-version message and leave the file untouched (REQ-029). Projects saved by 1.4.x open in 1.5.0 with the same result (no picture effects). Damaged values (scale outside 10%-400%, keyframe times outside the media, unknown shapes or fit modes, more than 200 keyframes) are corrected or refused without a crash (REQ-057). Clip keyframes are stored in source time, so a trim or cut never deletes them: only keyframes outside the media are dropped on load (a keyframe outside the kept part stays in the file and is hidden, REQ-129). Acceptance: a 1.4.0 example project opens and exports as before; a 1.5.0 file is refused by 1.4.1 with its message; a scale of 0% or 1000% in the file is corrected to the limit without a crash.

## Key decisions
- Change aspect applies to the whole video (one frame size); a clip has its own shape only through REQ-326 (2.3.0), with the Fit and empty-space choices of the whole video.
- Scale and Move are keyframed; the change between keyframes is a straight line with no easing, like the 360 camera path; keyframes for other effects are out of scope (volume since 2.0.4 and 2.3.0, see [Effects and frame options](business-effects-frame.md)).
- Fit on width and Fit on height keep one side of the video and cut or pad the other, so the picture is never stretched. Fit on height cuts the sides when the video is wider than the frame (MINOR; the smaller picture with bars was not chosen).
- Subtitle lines and text items keep their place in the frame when the picture is moved or scaled; they are not keyframed.
- Positions and sizes are stored relative to the frame, so a change of frame size does not move them.
- Rejected: free-typed aspect ratios (custom ratio 1:4 to 4:1 came in 2.0.4), rotation, a crop tool (Crop removed in 2.3.0), a strength setting for the blur (blur choice came in 2.0.4).
- A whole-video Scale or Move is given to each clip as its own copy of the keyframes, so each clip can be changed alone (REQ-148, Andrea, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-21). Change aspect is not copied: it sets the project frame (MINOR).
- A 1.6.0 clip with its own Scale or Move while the whole video has one is converted to one combined path (REQ-149, Andrea 2026-11-19); the alternatives (dropping the whole-video value, baking the LUT into a second track) were not chosen.
- Clip Change aspect (2.3.0, Andrea 2026-11-22): the clip has the same Fit and empty-space choices as the whole video; the 2.0.4 clip row offered only the shape and a fixed Fit on height.

## Known limitations
- On a 360 video export Change aspect, Scale and Move are ignored; the flat camera view applies them (REQ-131).
- The longer side of an output frame is capped at 4096 px (assumption): a Change aspect on a large source can give a frame that is scaled down, and a large frame exports more slowly (time not measured).
- Keyframes are straight lines, with no easing unless set per segment (2.0.4), and cannot be copied from one clip to another.
- Keyframes are shown in the popup and as the clip's marker, not as separate markers on the timeline.
- The blur of Fit empty with blurred video has one fixed strength (blur levels per clip in REQ-284).
- Subtitle lines and text items do not follow Scale or Move.
- Each copy of a whole-video Scale or Move is changed in its own clip's popup; a change of one copy does not reach the others (REQ-148).
- The picture handles (Scale corners, Move target) edit the clip under the playhead only (REQ-148).
- A converted 1.6.0 clip (REQ-149): the picture is exact at the kept keyframe times and a straight line between them, so it can differ from 1.6.0 in between.

## Out of scope
- Keyframes for effects other than Scale, Move and volume.
- Rotation; stabilisation; transitions.
