# Business — Overlap media (2.3.0)

Back to the [index](business.md). Planned for 2.3.0 from Andrea's request of 2026-11-22. **Overlap media** replaces **Add Picture** (REQ-287) as the way to put a picture over the video. Unlike a picture item, an overlap belongs to one video clip. Requirements REQ-325 and REQ-327 to REQ-331 (the Add media button is REQ-324 in [Editing](business-editing.md); the clip's own Change aspect is REQ-326 in [Frame](business-frame.md)).

## Features (planned)
- **Overlap media** in the Effects list and the Edit menu: pick a file with transparency (PNG, GIF, MOV, or another video with an alpha channel that the bundled ffmpeg reads). It is placed over one video clip.
- **One clip only**: the overlap is shown only while its clip plays, and moves, trims or is removed with it (REQ-329, decided 2026-11-22).
- **Start and end on the timeline**: the overlap has its own lane; its two ends are dragged on the timeline.
- **Place on the picture**: dragged in the preview, as text items are.
- **Size and ratio**: a MOV keeps its shape; a GIF or PNG keeps its shape too. Only Ctrl (Command on macOS) while dragging an edge changes the shape: a MOV is then cropped to the box, a GIF or PNG is stretched to it.
- **Animations**: a GIF or MOV plays once from the overlap's start, then is transparent; a PNG is still (REQ-328, decided 2026-11-22).
- **Export**: burned in at its place with its transparency; the preview and the export show the same picture.

## Requirements
- REQ-325 (implemented): The Effects list row **Overlap media** and the Edit menu entry replace **Add Picture** (REQ-287); the row shares the first row of the Effects list with **Add Text**. The row adds an overlap of a chosen file at the playhead, over the video clip under the playhead; with no video clip under the playhead nothing is added and the status line says so. Accepted files: PNG, GIF, MOV and other formats with an alpha channel that the bundled ffmpeg decodes; a file without an alpha channel is accepted and drawn opaque (a known limit), and a broken file is refused with a message (REQ-002). The overlap is 3 s long by default, cut to the clip. The overlap lane appears only while overlaps exist; all overlaps share this one lane, each a bar whose two ends are dragged; overlaps that overlap in time are placed on sub-rows, one to three visible at a time, and the mouse wheel scrolls to the others (accepted by Andrea); the overlap never starts before or ends after its clip (MINOR). Its sound, if a MOV has one, is not played (MINOR). Acceptance: an overlap added over clip 2 at 2 s shows from 2 s for 3 s; dragging its end to 5 s makes it stop at 5 s in the preview and in the export; dragging its start before the clip's start stops it at the clip start; a broken file is refused with its message and nothing is added.
- REQ-327 (implemented): The overlap's box in the preview: dragging the box moves the overlap (its centre stays on the frame, as REQ-117); dragging an edge or corner resizes it, the opposite side or corner staying fixed (as REQ-145). Without a modifier the shape never changes: the box keeps the media's own ratio. With Ctrl (Command on macOS) held while an edge is dragged, the box may change its ratio: a MOV is cropped to the box, keeping its ratio and cutting the overflow evenly from both sides (as REQ-125); a GIF or PNG is stretched to the box. One drag is one undo step. Acceptance: a 16:9 MOV dragged with Ctrl to a square box fills the square with no squeeze and the sides cut; a 4:3 PNG dragged with Ctrl to a square is stretched to the square; the same PNG dragged without Ctrl keeps 4:3; one undo restores the box.
- REQ-328 (implemented): A GIF or MOV overlap plays its animation once, from its start, and is transparent after the animation ends (decided by Andrea, 2026-11-22). A PNG is a still picture shown for the overlap's whole length. Acceptance: a 1 s animated GIF in a 3 s overlap shows the animation during the first second and is transparent for the last two; a PNG shows unchanged for its whole length.
- REQ-329 (implemented): Editing a clip that has overlaps (decided by Andrea, 2026-11-22; deviations accepted by Andrea). The overlap moves with its clip when the timeline ripples. A change of the clip's speed (Change speed, REQ-281) rescales the overlap's times with the clip's times. A split keeps each part of every overlap on the half of the clip it falls in, with no popup. A trim cuts each overlap to the kept part; the popup appears only when an overlap would have nothing left, and it says how many overlaps will be removed (OK removes them with the trim; Cancel cancels the trim). Deleting a clip shows one popup for all of its overlaps: OK deletes the clip and removes all its overlaps; Cancel keeps both. One undo restores the edit and the removed overlaps. Acceptance: an overlap from 2 s to 6 s on a 0 s to 8 s clip, the clip split at 4 s: the first part keeps 2 s to 4 s and the second part 4 s to 6 s, no popup; deleting the clip with two overlaps shows one popup saying two overlaps will be removed; Cancel leaves the clip and both overlaps; OK removes both; one undo restores them.
- REQ-330 (implemented; amends REQ-287; decided by Andrea, 2026-11-22): Picture items of older projects (Add Picture, REQ-287 of 2.0.4) each become an overlap on the video clip under their start, with the same place and times; an item with no clip under its start stays a picture item as it is. The conversion reports the opening status line of REQ-332, which also names the pictures made overlaps (wording confirmed by Andrea). Acceptance: a 2.2.0 project with one picture item at 2 s on clip 1 opens with one overlap on clip 1 at 2 s, the same picture and place; a project with no picture items opens unchanged.
- REQ-331 (implemented; amends REQ-287 and uses REQ-316): Overlap media elsewhere in the program: the overlap is a movable row of the picture group of the Applied effects widget (REQ-320), on top by default (MINOR); the preview and the export show it at the same place, within 2% of the frame height (as REQ-287); any overlap makes the export a re-encode (REQ-023); a 360 export does not draw overlaps (as it does not draw picture items); an overlay file above 1920 x 1080 is previewed at half size, while the export keeps its full size (decided by Andrea, 2026-11-22); a missing file stops the export with its message and the project still opens (REQ-287); the 16 megapixel limit of REQ-316 applies to the frame size of every overlap file (MINOR); a MOV without transparency is drawn opaque (a known limit). Acceptance: the widget lists the overlap in the picture group and its order can be changed (REQ-320); a PNG overlap moves, the export has it at the same place; a 4001 x 4000 PNG is refused with the REQ-316 message.

## Key decisions (2.3.0)
- (Andrea 2026-11-22) An overlap is bound to one clip; it is not a free object on the picture. This keeps the one-clip rule of Change aspect and of the clip effects.
- (Andrea 2026-11-22) Ctrl (Command) is the only way to change the shape of an overlap; a MOV is cropped and a GIF or PNG is stretched, as Andrea wrote.
- (MINOR) Add media (REQ-324) is the same action as File > Import media…, with a button at the end of the Media section.

## Out of scope (2.3.0)
- Overlaps on separate video tracks (multiple video tracks stay out of scope). Several overlaps on one clip are allowed (MINOR reading: the rule is the binding to one clip, not a limit of one overlap per clip).
- Sound of a MOV overlap; keyframes of an overlap (roadmap: keyframes for other effects).
- Shapes; rotation of an overlap.

## Known limitations (2.3.0)
- A MOV without an alpha channel is drawn opaque; the program does not check the alpha channel before adding it.
- Overlap media in a 360 export is not drawn.
