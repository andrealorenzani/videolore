Changes since the last release, newest first. Each entry: version and date-time. On release the entries become `releases/<version> - <name>/RELEASE_NOTES.md` and this file is cleaned.

## 2.0.3 — 2026-11-20 22:05 +0000
- Changed: business plan for 2.0.3 (see [editing](business-editing.md), [text](business-text.md), [subtitles](business-subtitles.md) and [subtitle accuracy](business-subtitle-accuracy.md)), one development for the batches Readable text, Subtitle placement and Subtitle accuracy, all planned in the business plan and built in this version: REQ-155 the paused picture in the last ~20 ms of a clip (fixes the 2.0.2 defect against REQ-144); REQ-156 to REQ-167 readable text (measured best fit, a 10% size limit for short texts, outline or background box, box height handle, live width readout, left-edge resize, box hidden while blinking, preview with the installed font, font fallback note, font names, cost of installed fonts, status message for migrated sizes); REQ-168 to REQ-176 subtitle and text placement (moves back inside after an edit, warnings for overlapping lines, click cycling through stacked boxes, selection hints, group drags of mixed selections, align on the style's place, centre kept when text wraps, Move inside the picture); REQ-177 to REQ-179 subtitle accuracy (the reused-subtitle target of REQ-095 on the reference clip, pieces without lost words, a message when a video's subtitle text does not follow the speech).
- Changed: architecture plan for 2.0.3 (see [text](architecture-text.md) and [subtitles](architecture-subtitles.md)): project format 10, with format 9 projects migrated on opening; the measured best fit of text sizes (no longer the 0.6 em estimate); text readability None, Outline or Box, with the preview and the export matching; placement rules and warnings for moved subtitle lines and text items; subtitle accuracy pieces without lost words; the paused-picture fix (REQ-155); step 0 is the CI fix below.
- Changed: projects saved by 2.0.3 are format 10: opening a format 9 project and saving it writes format 10, which 2.0.2 and older refuse with a message and leave untouched.
- Changed: texts of older projects open with Readability None and their sizes are refitted to the measured best fit; a one-time status message says so.
- Removed: the old text outline; texts that had it open with Readability None.
- Fixed: CI of commit e32376d (2.0.2) failed in `tests/ffmpeg_integration/v15.rs` (`a_4_3_video_in_a_tall_frame_with_a_colour_fill`) on the pinned ffmpeg 9.0.2, because of the boxblur chroma radius of the blurred fill; fixed in 2.0.3 (step 0 of the architecture plan: colour and blurred fill on small pictures).
- Added: text Readability (None, Outline or Box, in the Add Text popup and the text's popup), a box height handle, a live width readout while a text box is resized, a left-edge handle, and a subtitle-accuracy message when a video's subtitle text does not follow the speech (REQ-179); subtitle lines and text items show warnings for overlaps and partly outside the picture, with Move inside the picture in the Context Menu and the Edit menu.
- Changed: code for readable text (measured best fit with the font the picture uses, size limit of 10% of the picture height, box hidden while blinking), for placement (boxes move back inside after an edit, click cycling through stacked boxes, group drags of mixed selections, centre kept when text wraps) and for subtitle accuracy (consecutive recognition pieces overlap and a word heard twice is kept once, REQ-178); project format 10 writer and migration of format 9 and older projects.
- Changed: REQ-177 (the accuracy target of reused subtitles at the Fast level) stays planned and is not met: the speech model mishears 5 of 28 words on the reference clip, and DTW timestamps, a one-sided onset snap and a speech-region method were measured and not shipped. The strict test stays ignored; the decision for the maintainer is in the roadmap, Priority 1.
- Fixed: a paused picture is shown in the last ~20 ms of a clip (REQ-155, the 2.0.2 defect against REQ-144).
