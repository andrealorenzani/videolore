Rust libraries
  The Rust libraries compiled into videolore are listed, with their licence
  texts, in "rust-crates.txt" (shown in the About screen).

Source code
  Every release includes "videolore-<version>-third-party-source.tar.xz" with the
  FFmpeg source of the bundled builds, the build scripts at the pinned commits
  (which name and pin every library), the Noto Sans font reference and licence,
  and a README. Written offer: for at least three years after the release, the
  author will provide, on request, a copy of the corresponding source code of
  any GPL or LGPL component shipped in a videolore installer, for no more than
  the cost of physically performing the distribution. Ask through the project's
  issue tracker.

Patents
  H.264 (AVC) and H.265 (HEVC) are covered by patents in some countries. Whether
  you need a patent licence to encode or distribute such videos depends on where
  you live and what you do with them; videolore does not grant one.
