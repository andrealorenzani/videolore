# Business — Applied effects and their order

Back to the [index](business.md). Built in 2.1.0; changed in 2.2.0 (column, all effects movable) and 2.3.0 (Crop removed, Overlap media, Change speed fixed). The effects themselves are in [Effects](business-effects.md), [Text](business-text.md), [Frame](business-frame.md) and [Effects and frame options](business-effects-frame.md).

Before 2.1.0 the picture was built in a fixed order and nothing let the user choose it. The Applied effects widget lists the effects of the selected clip and lets the user choose the order of the movable rows.

## Features
- **Applied effects column**: on the right of the preview, about 15% of its width (at least 180 px), listing the effects of the selected clip.
- **Order**: the top row is applied last (drawn on top); the lower rows are applied first.
- **Reorder** inside a group: Move up / Move down, drag, or Alt+Up / Alt+Down.
- **Whole-video effects** are a copy on each clip; the widget lists only the clip's own effects, with no mark (REQ-303).

## Requirements

### Applied effects widget
- REQ-302 (implemented): The preview has an **Applied effects** widget (a column since 2.2.0, REQ-317). It lists the effects of the selected clip, or of the clip at the playhead when none is selected (assumption). Rows follow REQ-320. Each row shows its icon, its name and, for texts, the time range; a click opens its popup. With no clip the widget says "Select a clip to see its effects". The widget scrolls when its rows do not fit. Acceptance: on a clip with Apply LUT, then one text added: the text row is above the LUT row; a clip with no effects shows only the empty text; a click on the LUT row opens the Apply LUT popup; at 800x500 with the Preview section at its minimum every row is reachable by scrolling.
- REQ-303 (implemented): The widget lists the effects of the clip only, with no whole-video mark: a whole-video effect is a copy on each clip (REQ-147), and each copy is an ordinary clip row. The 2.1.0 inferred mark is removed. Acceptance: after a double-click of Volume on clips 1 to 3, each clip's widget shows its Volume row without any mark; a change of the Volume on clip 2 leaves the rows of clips 1 and 3 unchanged.

### Order and reorder
- REQ-304 (implemented): All effects are movable inside their group (REQ-320). The order is chosen in the widget with **Move up** and **Move down** buttons, by drag, or with Alt+Up and Alt+Down while a row has the focus or the pointer. A row higher up is applied later and drawn on top. The movable rows are Apply LUT, each text item, each picture item and the subtitle lines of the clip. The fixed rows (Change speed and the others of REQ-320 outside their group) keep their place. The order belongs to the clip: a text overlapping two clips can be above the LUT in one and below it in the other. A new LUT or picture item is put on top once the edit is done, so a LUT grades the texts; a new text is on top too. Each move is one undo step named after it (REQ-074, for example "Undo Move LUT down"; a drag is "Move effect"). Acceptance: a text above a LUT on a clip shows the text in its own colours in the preview and in the export; the same text moved below the LUT shows the LUT colours; one undo restores the order; a text overlapping clips 1 and 2 with the LUT only on clip 2 gets the order of clip 2 there and no change on clip 1.
- REQ-305 (implemented): Texts overlapping on the picture: the lower row is covered by the higher row where the two boxes overlap, in the preview and in the export. Moving a row swaps which text covers which. Projects saved before 2.1.0 keep the drawing order they have (a later text is drawn on top of an earlier one; accepted by Andrea in 2.2.0). Acceptance: two overlapping texts, A above B: A covers B in the preview and export; after Move down on A, B covers A in both; a 2.0.4 project with overlapping texts exports the same picture as before.

### Project file
- REQ-311 (implemented): The project stores, for each clip, the order of its movable rows (REQ-304; all effects since 2.2.0, REQ-322), and the settings of REQ-306 to REQ-310 (shadows, highlight colours, the Enlarge word size, the new styles). Project format 12 (2.1.0), 13 (2.2.0). Projects saved by 2.0.4 or earlier open with the same look and export (the defaults of the edge cases). A newer file is refused by older versions with their newer-version message, left untouched (REQ-029). A damaged order entry is dropped with the line "A damaged effect order was repaired" in the "Some files could not be found or were repaired" notice, without a crash (REQ-057). Acceptance: a 2.0.4 example project opens and exports as before; a damaged order entry is dropped with that line in the notice, without a crash.

## Edge cases and rules for the builders
- **Old projects** have no stored order; they open with the order the picture has today (LUT below the texts and subtitle lines; texts in their present stacking), so their look and export do not change (REQ-311, REQ-322).
- **Render order**: fixed rows keep the order of the render graph; only the place of the movable rows relative to each other and to the picture layers changes. A LUT is always applied after Scale, Move and Change aspect, as today.
- **Parity**: preview and export use the same order; the export is a re-encode when the order differs from the default (REQ-023).
- **360 clips**: the order applies to anchored texts and anchored subtitle lines (REQ-257), which are fixed rows. A 360 video export (the whole sphere) ignores the order; the flat camera view keeps it.
- **Blink**: a hidden text (REQ-162) takes no place in the picture; its row stays in the widget.
- **Shadow and readability**: the order decides whether a LUT colours a text's shadow and outline too (REQ-306).
- **Undo**: each reorder is one undo step; the widget itself does not change the project.

## Layout and the order of all effects (2.2.0, amended 2.3.0)
- REQ-317 (implemented): Amends REQ-302. The widget is a **vertical column on the right side of the Preview section**, beside the picture. Its width is about 15% of the Preview section's width, never less than 180 px (at 1280 x 800 the column is 180 px, about 20%). Its rows scroll vertically. The picture takes the rest of the section and keeps its aspect ratio; when the section is narrower than the picture's minimum plus the column's minimum, the picture shrinks first (REQ-068) and the column keeps its minimum width. Its width follows the section's border and is remembered in the saved layout. Popups are floating windows and are not placed inside the column. Acceptance: at 1280 x 800 the picture and the column are side by side and the column is about 15% of the section's width; at the minimum window size the column is 180 px; a list of 20 rows scrolls inside the column; a dragged border changes the width and it is the same after a restart.
- REQ-320 (implemented; amended 2.3.0: Crop removed, Overlap media is a picture row, Change speed is the fixed row): Amends REQ-302 and REQ-304. Every effect of a clip can be reordered in the widget. The widget shows one list with two groups and one fixed row:
  - **Picture group** (top row applied last, drawn on top): Change aspect (the clip's frame), Apply LUT, texts, picture items, Overlap media (REQ-325), subtitle lines, Fade IN/OUT, Scale, Move. Their order is the order of the picture build.
  - **Sound group**: Clean audio and Volume (REQ-280). Their order is the order of the sound build.
  - **Change speed**: a fixed row at the bottom, applied first (REQ-281); it sets the clip's time, so every other row is applied to the clip's new timing.
  A row moves only inside its group. The default order of an old project and of a new clip is the order the picture and the sound have today; an old project with no stored order keeps it (REQ-311). Examples: Move before Scale and after Scale give different places; a LUT above Fade IN/OUT keeps the fade black where the LUT maps black to black, a LUT below the fade grades it; Volume above Clean audio raises the level before the noise reduction, below it after. No order is refused. Fade IN/OUT is a movable picture row whose time is the clip's timeline time after Speed. Decided (Andrea, 2.2.0): Speed is fixed and applied first; the LUT's place against Change aspect and Fade IN/OUT is the user's choice (moving the LUT above Change aspect grades the frame fill). Built: the Fade row shows as "Fade IN/OUT" only when the clip has several fades; a text on a reordered clip is drawn through a matte, which is slower. Acceptance: moving Volume above Clean audio gives a different sound in the preview and the export (test); Change speed cannot be moved; a row cannot be dropped in the other group; the default order of a 2.1.0 project is unchanged in the export (golden test).
- REQ-321 (implemented; amended 2.3.0): Undo, parity and copies. Each move is one undo step, named after the row as REQ-304 does ("Undo Move Volume up"; a drag is "Move effect"). Preview and export use the same chain (REQ-131 parity). Placement: a new text, picture item, Overlap media or LUT goes on top of the other movable rows; a new Change aspect, Scale, Move or Fade takes its default place in the picture group, not the top (on top, a Scale above Change aspect would scale the background); a new sound row keeps the default order Clean audio then Volume. A whole-video effect (REQ-147) puts a copy on each clip, each taking its default place on that clip; a later whole-video change does not reorder the copies. 360 clips: the texts and lines fixed to the sphere keep fixed rows; the 360 video export ignores the order. Acceptance: moving a copy on clip 2 does not move the copy on clip 1; a new Scale takes its default place; one undo restores the order; the preview and the export agree on a test frame.
- REQ-322 (implemented; amended 2.3.0): Amends REQ-311. The project stores the order of every movable row of each clip (REQ-320); Change speed is not stored in the order because it is fixed, its value is stored with the clip (REQ-281); a stored Crop row is dropped (REQ-332). A project saved before 2.2.0 has no stored order for the new rows and opens with the default order: its look and export do not change. Project format 13 (ADR-222): 2.1.0 and older refuse it with their newer-version message and leave the file untouched (REQ-029); formats 12 and older still open. A damaged order entry is dropped with the 2.1.0 line (REQ-311). Acceptance: a 2.1.0 example project opens and exports as before; a 2.2.0 project with Volume moved above Clean audio opens again with that order.

## Key decisions
- (2.1.0, MINOR) The movable rows are Apply LUT, texts, picture items and subtitle lines; the other effects keep their place, because moving them would change many existing outputs. The order is per clip, not per timeline (the widget shows one clip; a whole-video LUT is a copy on each clip). A new text or LUT is applied last (top).
- (2.2.0, Andrea 2026-11-21, GO) Every effect can be reordered inside its group; Speed is fixed. The picture and the sound act on different chains, so their relative order is not offered. Speed is fixed and applied first; Fade IN/OUT stays movable in the picture group; today's order is the default for old projects and new clips (MINOR, no old output changes); widget width 15% of the section, at least 180 px (MINOR).
- (2.2.0, Andrea 2026-11-21) Whole-video effects are distributed as copies and the whole-video mark is removed (REQ-303, REQ-147). REQ-305 is accepted as built: old projects keep their stacking; a new LUT or picture item goes on top.
- (2.3.0, Andrea 2026-11-22) Crop is removed (REQ-283); Speed is the fixed Change speed row (REQ-281); Volume is a keyframed effect in the Sound group (REQ-280); Overlap media is a picture row on top by default (REQ-325, REQ-331).

## Known limitations
- Overlapping subtitle lines (REQ-169) are not ordered by the widget; subtitle lines of one clip keep their own order.
- Texts that overlap several clips can have a different order on each clip; the widget shows the order of the selected clip only.
- Texts and lines fixed to the sphere of a 360 clip keep fixed rows (decision Q-3, 2.1.0).
- A 360 video export (the whole sphere) ignores the order.
- Subtitle lines that go to a separate subtitle track are not drawn by the clip, so the order does not apply to them.

## Out of scope
- Ordering of individual subtitle lines in the widget (see the [roadmap](roadmap.md)); layers on the timeline (multiple video tracks stay out of scope).
- Keyframes for the order, and a separate order for each word.
