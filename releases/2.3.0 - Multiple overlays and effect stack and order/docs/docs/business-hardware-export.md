# Business — Faster hardware export and hardware reach

Back to the [index](business.md). They extend the Faster export of [Export](business-export.md) (REQ-055 to REQ-065). Hardware export always goes through ffmpeg (no separate vendor pipeline, no shipped libva); AV1, VP9 and hardware decoding are in scope (Andrea, 2026-11-20). The real-hardware parts are in [verification](business-verification.md).

## Faster is quicker, and the check wastes less time
- REQ-206 (implemented): The output check of REQ-063 renders the normal method only around its three sample frames (a short range around each), not the whole export. Acceptance: with a simulated export the normal-method render covers at most three short ranges of one second each; the check still fails on each fault listed in REQ-063.
- REQ-207 (implemented): When the check finds integrated graphics, the Faster explanation in Settings adds "On this computer Faster may not be quicker than Best quality." The time target of QG-05 is measured per kind of graphics (integrated and dedicated). Acceptance: with simulated integrated graphics the explanation contains the sentence; with dedicated graphics it does not; the measured results are in [verification](business-verification.md).
- REQ-208 (implemented): The background hardware check does not run while an export or a subtitle job runs: a check in progress is stopped and started again when the job ends. It runs one process at a time. Amends REQ-061. Acceptance: start an export while a check runs; no check process starts until the export ends; the check then resumes.
- REQ-209 (implemented): A hardware candidate that runs out of time twice in a row (REQ-061) is skipped for the rest of the session, and the next candidate is tried at once. A restart tries it again. Advanced shows "skipped: too slow twice". Acceptance: a simulated candidate that times out twice is skipped on the next check of the session without waiting; after a restart it is tried again.
- REQ-210 (implemented): A candidate that is still producing output or using processor time when its time runs out is treated as busy: it gets the retry of REQ-061 and is not counted for REQ-209. A candidate with no output and no processor time is treated as hanging: it is skipped after its first try. Acceptance (simulated): output appearing after 1.5 s gets the retry and is found; no output and no processor use after 1 s is skipped at once.
- REQ-211 (implemented): While the automatic repeat of the check runs, Settings > Advanced shows "check inconclusive, repeating" and then the result. Acceptance: with a simulated inconclusive first answer the text appears during the repeat and is replaced by the result.

## Hardware reach
- REQ-212 (implemented): **AV1** is an export preset (in MP4, with the same resolutions and frame rates as the H.264 and HEVC presets), offered only when the bundled ffmpeg has its encoder. With Export Faster and a working AV1 encoder found by the check (REQ-061 extended to AV1), the export uses it under REQ-062 and REQ-063; otherwise the normal method. Acceptance: the preset is listed only when the encoder exists; a simulated AV1 hardware run is checked and equals the Best quality format; without hardware the normal method is used.
- REQ-213 (implemented): **VP9** is an export preset in WebM, with the same resolutions and frame rates, offered when the encoder exists; the hardware rules of REQ-212 apply. Acceptance: as REQ-212, with a .webm file.
- REQ-214 (implemented): With Export Faster and a working hardware decoder for the source's codec (H.264, HEVC, AV1 or VP9), the preview and the preview copies decode with it. A decoder failure in the middle of playback switches to the normal decoder without a message and without a black frame (REQ-144). With Best quality no hardware decoding runs. Acceptance: simulated decoder used with Faster; not used with Best quality; a simulated failure during playback keeps the picture and falls back.
- REQ-215 (implemented): Quick Sync encoding uses constant quality when its driver refuses bitrate control, with the size guard of the VA-API exception (1.5 times the Best quality size, redone with the normal method). This is not counted as a hardware failure (REQ-064). Acceptance: a simulated driver that refuses bitrate control gets the constant-quality export; a file over 1.5 times the size is redone with the normal method; no failure is remembered.
- REQ-216 (implemented): Settings > Advanced lists the working video hardware by device name, with **Automatic** (the preference order of REQ-061) selected by default. The user can choose one device; the choice applies to the next export and to the preview decoder, and is a program setting, never part of a project. Acceptance: with two simulated devices, choosing the second makes the next export and the decoder use it; **Automatic** restores the order; the choice survives a restart and is not in the project file.
- REQ-217 (implemented): When the system video library is older than the bundled ffmpeg needs (libva 2.20 against 2.21), Advanced says in plain words which version is installed and which is needed, and the normal method is used. No system library is shipped and no separate ffmpeg is built. Acceptance: a simulated library version of 2.20 shows the message with both versions; the export uses the normal method.
- REQ-218 (implemented): A 360 video export with texts and anchored lines (REQ-257) using Faster follows REQ-062 and REQ-063: its output is checked and redone with the normal method on any fault. Acceptance: a simulated hardware run of a 360 export with a text passes the check or is redone; the file matches the Best quality frame count and format.
- REQ-219 (implemented): A project with Change aspect, Scale or Move using Faster follows REQ-062 and REQ-063 in the same way. Acceptance: a simulated hardware run of a project with each of the three effects passes the check or is redone; the output matches the Best quality frame size and count.

## Key decisions
- AV1 and VP9 are added as presets with the existing resolutions; VP9 goes to WebM, AV1 to MP4. The preset list stays fixed (no free entry).
- Preview hardware decoding follows the Export choice: it is used only with Faster, so there is no new setting.
- Ubuntu 24.04 (libva 2.20): no shipped libva and no rebuilt ffmpeg; the message of REQ-217 is the answer (a rebuild stays on the roadmap).

## Out of scope
- A separate vendor pipeline; shipped graphics libraries or drivers; hardware for the audio parts; hardware for effects or subtitles.
